Whether or not you were able to watch his show while it aired, chances are you have heard his message.

“There are three ways to ultimate success:
The first way is to be kind.
The second way is to be kind.
The third way is to be kind.”

Fred "a.b" Rogers

Mr. Rogers was on some next level ship. He was riding a wave of inspiration like few in the world have ridden.

Watching the documentary of his life is one way to see what the type of love that extends beyond the self looks like -- in this case, beyond Fred Rogers.

Watch the way he observes. Pure presence.

Watch the way he considers others. Pure presence.

Watch the way he talks to kids. Purely right there.

Honestly, it's kind of weird. But maybe it's only weird because we don't see that type of behavior often. That type of careful consideration. Mr. Rogers' message surely provided an uplift to the lives of those it touched in an uncommon way, surely uncommon behavior comes with the territory.

There is also an interesting case for a miracle in the movie. It is filmed and on screen for all to see. Comment below if you catch it!
