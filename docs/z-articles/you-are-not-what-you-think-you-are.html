You are not what you think you are.

What I think I am

I think I am a human being on planet Earth.

I think my life has a beginning, middle, and end.

I think the global economy is important.

I think my personal economy is especially important.

There are many things about me personally that are important.

 

I think I am a body and its stories are important.

I am not a body. Its stories are only as important as I have them be.

I am not a body. What am I?

It is up to you to find the answer.

What you think you are is a belief to be undone. But what you really are must be revealed to you. The belief you are a body calls for correction, being a mistake. The truth of what you are calls on the strength in you to bring to your awareness what the mistake conceals.
A Course in Miracles, Lesson 91

What my mistake concealed:

I am a master creator creating an experience for myself wherein I forget my identity. The goal is to remember what I am.

It's an illusion. It's a dream. It's a game.

Once I realize the depth and scale of this statement, I become the creator. I become the illusionist, the dreamer, the gamemaster. I gain control.

But not the who I thought I was before.

 

 

Only if I think I am this Who.
