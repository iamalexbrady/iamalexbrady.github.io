A gentle answer: "It is not so."

1.

One walks with you Who gently answers all your fears with this one merciful reply, "It is not so." He points to all the gifts you have each time the thought of poverty oppresses you, and speaks of His Companionship when you perceive yourself as lonely and afraid.

ACIM Workbook Lesson 166

vision

An inward recognition of the holiness within all things, which overlooks bodies and their attacks. This perception comes not through our eyes but from a spiritual faculty in us called the eyes of Christ. Rather than seeing visual forms or color, it “sees” pure meaning, the meaning of holiness.
