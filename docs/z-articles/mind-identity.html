Mind Identity

I've been kicking around the idea from A Course in Miracles regarding the concept of identity.

I am mind. I am spirit. I am not a body. I am free. I am as God created me.

 

I've gathered some of my thoughts in this powerpoint:  https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0281/7887/4403/files/Identity_v2.pdf?v=1605626169 This is not the finished product, but it's what I have now.

Note: I have extracted most religious terminology away from the ideas as presented in the Course (Father, Son, you know the story..). The story that is used to convey understsndings in the Course becomes a useful learning device eventually, but it is not necessary for foundational and practical identity principles.

A more finished product can only be enhance by other's insights, so if you read this and have strong ideas or feelings leave a comment or contact me:  iamalexbrady@gmail.com.
