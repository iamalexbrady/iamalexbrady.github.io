Why A Course in Miracles is Great 1

I am alex brady and this is why A Course in Miracles is great.

  1. It teaches the learner how to do miracles.

    If you wanted to learn calculus, you would take a course in calculus.

  2. It's 100% self-guided and egalitarian. No weird positions of power. (unless that's something you're about ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°))

    Feel free to join the club and share your newly acquired understandings. Share your light.

  3. The teachings make you feel more happy without prescribing morality.

    This is a course on how to acquire a peaceful state of mind. One of joy, honesty, and freedom.

    Are you happy right now or not? Would you like a path out?
