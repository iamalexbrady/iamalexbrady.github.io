ACIM Urtext

{{ page.title }}

Presented are is the ACIM Urtext. The Urtext is the first typewritten draft of ACIM – it reflects what Helen Schucman dictated to Thetford. It was a version of ACIM considered "private" and "not intended for publication" in its pre-edited state. Indeed, reading it does give one the sense that you are peeking into a very private, very personal meeting between the Voice and the Scribes.

Enjoy.
