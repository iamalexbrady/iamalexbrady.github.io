The world is make-believe. From this conversation to the one occurring in the [oval office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oval_Office). 

**The behaviors of the actors ain't real. Not a real portrayal of Reality.**

A) The behaviors are prescribed. The actor will always do what they think they *should* do.

From the actors perspective, the behavior seems to arise as spontaneously as their awareness of their movement through time. 

E.g. - their awareness of time is 1 awareness per minute. Their attention is on decision 1 time per minute.

Conventional free will is thought of as spontaneous decision making. It is not truly free will because the algorithm is *always* "what i think i should do".

To the actor this seems to spontaneously arise only because they are not aware of future space-time. But given every physical and personality variable -- unknown to the actor perhaps, but existing and operating -- the decision "what I think I should do" is prescribed.
