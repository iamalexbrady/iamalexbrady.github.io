**Him: I'm ooold.**

Me- how old?

**Him: All of it.**

Christ is all of it years old. Being life itself, he is all lifeforms simultaneously. He's reading all of it at the same time. You can think of it as he is every lifeform at every moment.

(Visualize how much life is existing at any given moment. Multiply that by the number of moments.)

This is enough for stage 1 understanding.

When you are ready (aka able) to recognize that *nothing* is as it seems, add this idea: time is happening all at once as well.

Next, consider this: time isn't real at all. Christ Mind simply *knows* all of time, and hasn't aged one bit. The concept of "age" necessarily requires time which, as stated, isn't real at all.
