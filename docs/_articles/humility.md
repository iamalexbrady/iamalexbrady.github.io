You have no idea what's going on in front of you right now. That's not speaking poorly of you; it's very literally how we built the system.

We put something *unlike* us in front of us for examination purposes.

The world of perception is **unlike** us, what we are at the next level.

As much context, knowledge, informaiton, and expertise one may have about the perceptual world, There simply is another frame of mind from which to view the PETs<sup>1</sup> found here.

That's how far far out ACIM's teachings can take you. It talks a lot about perception not being real, but more importantly, following its provided exercises and training allows one to experience being at that level of awareness.

- alex brady

<sup>1</sup> - **PETs:** Persons, events, things, concepts
