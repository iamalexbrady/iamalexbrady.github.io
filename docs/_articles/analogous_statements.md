<div class="subtitle">
Recognizing where a statement can be generalized to include a broadened set of aspects (rather than the given specific) is a way to increase the meaning density of a given statement.
</div>

<hr>

## Example 1:

**Original Statement:** There is one Life and that I share with God.

There is one Love and that I share with God.

There is one Will and that I share with God.

There is one Peace and that I share with God.

There is one Joy and that I share with God.

There is one Life and that I share with All.

<hr>

## Example 2:

**Original Statement:**

Pretending not to be in contact with **the Mind of God** is pretending to be **impoverished.**<br>
**God Mind** is more **abundant** than even the
English word **"abundant"** can imply.
No linguistic symbol can convey the depth
of the **abundance** of **God.**<br>
What makes **God Mind “abundant”** is what makes
**linguistic symbols manifestly apparent.**

**Examples:**

To pretend not to be in contact with **Mind** is to pretend to be **vulnerable.**<br>
**Mind** is more **invulnerable** than even the
English word **"invulnerable"** can imply.
No linguistic symbol can convey the scale
of the **invinvibility** of **Mind.**<br>
**Mind's invulnerability** is how
**linguistics can be understood** at all.

You pretending not to be in contact with **Christ Mind** is you pretending to be **not-free.**<br>
**Christ Mind** is more free than even the
English word **"freedom"** can imply.
No linguistic symbol can convey the **freedom** of **Christ.**<br>
What makes **Christ Mind “free”** is what makes
**all things** -- all things real and seemingly real.

To pretend not to be in contact with **Higher Self** is to pretend to be **untalented.**<br>
**Higher Self** is more **talented** than even the
English word **"talented"** can imply.
No linguistic symbol can convey the products
of the **talents** of **Higher Self.**<br>
**Self's talents** are **Creation itself.**

Pretending not to be in contact with **the Holy Spirit** is pretending to be **unenlightened.**<br>
**The Holy Spirit** is more **enlightened** than even the
English word **"enlightened"** can imply.
No linguistic symbol can convey the enormity and clarity
of the **understandings** of **the Holy Spirit.**<br>
**The Holy Spirit's “enlightenment”** is **all knowing.**
