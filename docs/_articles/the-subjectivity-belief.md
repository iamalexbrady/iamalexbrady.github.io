## The belief in subjective perspective

This is a description of a phenomenon that is taken for granted
as the accurate perspective by which to describe systems of the world.

And not only describe.
It is a foundational premise by which we analyze, build, and
assume our part within all interactive contexts.
* Human conversation
* Computer Networking
* A co-related term is the "subject-object relationship".

The Subjectivity Principle has the following composition:

1. I am a knowing being with beliefs and experiences.
2. There are other knowing beings with a different set of beliefs.

This is what is professed by many to be "just the way things are."
I hope these two aspects of the belief are obvious.

Now I will make the not-so-obvious assertion:
There is a way to perceive the system of life that recognizes
beliefs and experience as a purely objective phenomenon.

While it may be that how we conventionally describe the world is
via Stories of US - you and me and interaction -
there is possibility and in actualizing of the possibility 
immense value in learning to understand and wield objective perspective.

This immense value comes in the form of Limitless Peace of Mind.
Limitless power and life. Perfect Happiness.

Given the Objectivity Belief, these attributes must be
the logical outcome of the system in which it operates.

Again, The Subjectivity Principle is a conventionally unnamed belief we took for granted.

1. I am a knowing being with beliefs and experiences.
2. There are other knowing beings with a different set of beliefs
   and experiences.

Now that this is named, I assert a valid and powerfiul position from
which to view the world wherein a thinking being
believes their self to be experiencing. This new perspective is of
an objective knowing in the Present Moment - The Act of Am.

I further assert concious mastery of the Act of Am yields the
positive presence of miracle working.
