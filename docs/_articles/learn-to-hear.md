You have no idea how the Holy Spirit speaks.

Yet.

When you understand how the Holy Spirit speaks
you will also understand that death is not real.

I am being a little facetious in that
"how the Holy Spirit speaks" and "death is not real"
are two distinct understandings that one can come to
at apparently separate times.

You do not need the one to get the other.
They can be gained independenty. 

Both are recognitions of a hyper-communicative oneness
aspect of our self that we are not well-versed in culturally.

Once you learn one, the other is as near as
an extension of the learned realization.

Life is free and eternal. Be well.

If you still need to learn this, listen. Learn to hear and listen.

\- alex brady
