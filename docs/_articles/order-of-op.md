That is the Order of Operations.

You see what you are looking for.

You think the world is coming at you.

The position which say you are simply reading what is inherently existent is incorrect. 

You are putting what you want to see in the world.

You decide what you want to see, then you proceed to have the things you see mean what you want to believe.

At the most tragic, we wish for death.

Yet in the light, it means life.

You must learn to get behind this dynamic. Grab this horse by the reins, and guide it.
