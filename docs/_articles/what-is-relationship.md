Relationship is how you *perceive* something.

How you understand it. What xperience it beings into your life whan you think of it.

You have a relationship with every person, place, thing, and concept.

Even if you haven't heard of a concept, thing or place, you have a relationship. A relationship of "forgetfulness."

## Article again

**Typo 1: xperience** Relationship is how you *perceive* something. What **xperience** it beings into your life whan you think of it.

X is Christ, like Xmas. Xperience is a cool looking word that now means Christ-sperience. Christ-sperience is synonymous with true perception.

**Typo 2: beings** Relationship is how you *perceive* something. What xperience it **beings** into your life whan you think of it.

To have and to be are synonymous in truth. This is more of an interesting Freudian slip that illuminates *beingness* as the medium of *having*.

**Typo 3: sentence fragments** How you understand it. What xperience it beings into your life whan you think of it. A relationship of "forgetfulness."

I do not know yet if there is significance with the sentence fragments, but I do see them as out of the ordinary for my writing style.
