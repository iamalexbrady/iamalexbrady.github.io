## steps to remembering god

1. hear god say "good morning everybody"
2. hear god say "good morning [your name]"
3. remember bodies aren't real

Why this works:

1. You identify as a body, so you identify as a being address with the first statement. You are acknowledged as one of many.
2. Once you hear the first, you've remembered you can be acknowledged. You are acknowledged as one of one.
3. Understanding bodies are not real is active awakened state. You have remembered God.
