A definitve survey of the theory and practical application of the A Course in Miracles literature.

We offer bite-sized courses to help the learner better visualize their progress. Each course is focused on one primary text of ACIM.

We stress that ACIM's final goal of "At-one-ment" is an attainable state. Tools and exercises are provided by alexbrady organization with the goal of the learner truly realizing such a state.

## The Curriculum

The Course in Miracles curriculum is a series of courses to be done in the following order:

**Theory 1:** Study of A Course in Miracles Text

**Practical 2:** Study of ACIM Workbook Part 1

**Practical 3:** Study of ACIM Workbook Part 2 and Manual

## Courses

### Theory 1

Learn core concepts and terminology for awakening principles.

**Goal:** Completion of the ACIM Text. The focus is completion of the text.

**Overview:** Read every section of every chapter of the A Course in Miracles text.

**Materials:** A Course in Miracles Text: Original Edition

**Resources:** Reading Schedule: Original Edition

### Practical Application 2

Put theory and concepts into practice. Use Miraculous Forgiveness to encourage the live experience of the Awakened Understanding. Train your mind to access this level at higher frequencies.

**Goal:** Loosening of egoic beliefs

**Overview:** Completion of the first 220 ACIM Workbook exercises with Forgiveness Track

**Materials:** A Course in Miracles Workbook

### Practical Application 3

**Goal:** Restoration of vision

**Overview:** ACIM Workbook Part 2 exercises

**Materials:** A Course in Miracles Workbook

### Creative Work

**Overview:** Guided training through creating a work of original content regarding Awakening.

**Goal:** Solidification and proliferation of the understanding.

**Price:** $300
