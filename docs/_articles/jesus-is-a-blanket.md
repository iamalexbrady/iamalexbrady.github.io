Jesus is a blanket of energy covering the earth. Because the earth isn't real, this is not a description of ultimate reality, but this is a fine model of perception and an idea towards true perception.

Just as there is a magnetic field, electric field, etc, there is a field of conciousness and life force itself.

Look outside, LOOK at the world your body's eyes can see. Imagine a fog in your immediate surroundings as well. Now color the fog purple. This is a fine way to think of it. This fog is the blanket.

Now remove any variance within the fog, no wind can move it, no spot is more dense than another. A perfectly uniform field. It extends through any perceived objects, such that if you see a car, the car's "atoms" do not displace the field.

This field exists in all instances of space, ß.

This is called the Jesus field because Jesus figured it out first and he currently resides there and only there. It is conciousness;  mind energy;  understandings;  mental and emotional content.

It is the medium of [conveyed understanding.](https://acim.iamalexbrady.com/acim/question/13-what-is-conveyed-understanding.html)

It is called the Jesus field, but it is where we all reside as well, beyond the body. We truly take up the full expanse of the field, but our influence is localized to our own imagination as untrained and ignorant minds. As we strengthen or perceive this field (spiritual/ mind training), our influence expands within this field.

It is not necessary to know this field exists to be a part of it. It is not necessary to intentionally train subjective awareness of it to increase influence here. The influence an "individual" has within this field varies from individual to individual. And throughout time.

The field is SET. An individual can only be in alignment or out of alignment. Imagine it as a vector space. The field's vectors are set and knowable. An individual's vector space may not align with the field's. This misalignment of vector spaces is a representation of misperception. The extent to which an individual's vectors are aligned, is the extent to which an individual's influence is greater. (Light shines brighter. Transparent to the truth. Etc)

Take time to "see" this space (Jesus field) with your body's eyes. The space is purple and extends in all the space around you. Now overlay the space with *your* understanding field. Your understanding field is either aligned with j-field and CLEAR, yielding a purple space. Or is it misaligned, yielding a darkening. Perfect perception is Jesus' purple.

An individual's understanding space overlayed with "Perfect" understanding does create seeming variance in the field. It is by noticing and aligning the misaligned vectors that the darkness is removed and the clarity shines through.

**Alternate View**

An alternative view of the colored visuals is that the field is BLUE and an individual's perception is RED. When understandings are aligned, they produce purple. Where there is misalignment, the subject only sees red.

J-field is purple because from his perspective, the whole field is purple. Untrained minds will see red and purple patchwork. The goal is to see purple.

The use of this model comes when differentiating knowledge and perception. Within perception, it is IMPOSSIBLE, to see the true color of the field, blue. You only know you are in alignment when the field is the intermediate color, purple. This exposes a further goal is seeking the true color, blue (i.e. the step from perception to knowledge.)

It is very possible, in fact inevitable, but as an individual you must first correct all (most) dark patches or red patches before the transfer can be made.

To be in the state of clarity, high influence, high alignment, is to live a life of joy inner peace and contentment regardless of outer perceptions. Your inner perceptions are aligned to truth, so nothing outer means anything other than peace joy and contentment. This can be accomplished even while seeing the body. Body-identity is just another red patch to bring into alignment eventually. Have patience and faith.

Coming into alignment with The Understandings can be done by living a lifetime and bumping against the trials and tribulations willy-nilly. In this way, correction from "red-to-purple" will occur, but slowly. A cleaner, faster method for strengthen your inner clarity through mind training. Once the mind is trained correction can occur at will, by concious desire.

This model of the blanket of Understandings covering all perceptual space is a truly helpful learning for the correction of misperceptions to true perception. The Inner Guide has the True vectors to which a seeming individual must align.

All the world is covered in a blanket ✅

If your Inner Guide is Jesus, Jesus is the Blanket ✅
